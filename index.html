MezTal Feedback Tool

Point-and-comment on the live site. Internal — George & Sarah.

Step 1 · Download

↓ Download the extension (.zip)

Unzip it. You'll get a folder called ext. Keep it somewhere permanent (not Downloads).

Step 2 · Add to Chrome (once)

  1. Open chrome://extensions
  2. Turn on Developer mode (top-right toggle)
  3. Click Load unpacked → pick the unzipped ext folder
  4. A MezTal Feedback icon appears in your toolbar (pin it via the puzzle-piece menu)

Step 2.5 · Turn on cloud backup (one-time, so nothing is ever lost)

  1. Right-click the MezTal Feedback icon → Options
  2. Follow the 4 steps there to paste a GitHub token (2 min) → Save

Now every note also saves to a private repo — survives cache clears, new browsers, and other computers. Skip this and it still auto-saves locally (just this browser).

Step 3 · Use it on the site

  1. Go to the live site
  2. Click the MezTal Feedback toolbar icon → a toolbar appears top-right
  3. Hit 📍 Point or ▢ Box, then click (or drag) on the page → type your note
  4. Every note lands in the ☰ List sidebar, grouped by page. Click an item to jump to its marker; click its text to expand.
  5. Hit ⤓ Export → it copies all your feedback. Paste it to George.
  6. Click the toolbar icon again (or ✕) to hide it.

Good to know

  • Notes auto-save in your browser as you type — reloads and crashes won't lose them.
  • Runs only when you click the icon, only on the tab you're on. Nothing is installed on the MezTal site.
  • Works on every page — markers stay organized page by page.
