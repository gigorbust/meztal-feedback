MezTal Feedback Tool

Point-and-comment on the live site. Internal — George & Sarah.

Step 1 · Install (once, ~15 sec)

Make sure your bookmarks bar is showing (Cmd+Shift+B), then drag this orange button up onto it:

📍 MezTal Feedback

Can't drag? Right-click it → Bookmark this link.

Step 2 · Use it on the site

  1. Go to the live site: my-site-ckn0ydnn-sthomas834.wix-vibe-site.com
  2. Click the MezTal Feedback bookmark. A toolbar appears top-right.
  3. Hit 📍 Point or ▢ Box, then click (or drag) on the page to drop a marker + type your note.
  4. Every note lands in the ☰ List sidebar, grouped by page. Click a list item to jump to its marker; click the text to expand it.
  5. When done, hit ⤓ Export — it copies all your feedback. Paste it to George.

Good to know

  • Your notes auto-save in your browser as you type — reloads and crashes won't lose them.
  • Works on every page of the site — markers stay organized page by page.
  • Hit ✕ in the toolbar to hide it anytime.
